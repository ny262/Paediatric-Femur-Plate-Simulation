## Paediatric Femur Plate Simulation
> [!WARNING]
> Active development: Core simulations and parameters updating.

### Summary
An anatomically conscious, highly adaptable, patient-specific femoral fixation plate, explicitly engineered to preserve bone microvascular blood supply while maximising mechanical stabilization within paediatric anatomical constraints.

### Project Breakdown
- 📐 **Design Tools:** Autodesk Fusion 360, mesh-to-solid modelling workflows, and clinical geometry extraction.
- 🧪 **Testing & Success Metrics:** Multi-axis adult gait telemetry downscaled to a 29 kg paediatric payload, incorporating a target structural safety factor of 2.0.
- 🧬 **Biomechanical Configurations (From Physiological Constraints):**
  - *Biological Preservation:* Scalloped underside minimises periosteal contact to protect outer blood flow and accelerate natural bone healing.
  - *Mechanical Versatility:* LCP Combi Holes integrate threaded fixed-angle sections with dynamic compression screw options for intraoperative load management.
